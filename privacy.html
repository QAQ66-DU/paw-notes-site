隱私政策 / Privacy

繁體中文English

繁體中文

本隱私政策適用於爪爪小記 App 與支援網站，生效日期 2026-10-10。

裝置上的日記

你不需建立帳號即可記錄寵物。寵物姓名、種類、可選生日／加入家庭日期／品種／性別／照片、餵食、散步、體重、照護紀錄、提醒及 備註儲存在裝置上的 App 資料空間；上次選擇的寵物、外觀與通知偏好也保存在裝置上。 目前 App 沒有自有伺服器同步、廣告或行為分析 SDK，也不會自動把日誌上傳給我們。

你選擇的照片

只有你選擇的照片會被讀取；App 使用系統照片選取器，不要求完整相簿或相機權限。 照片經縮小後以 JPEG 副本儲存在本機，移除來源定位及相機資訊。移除 App 內照片不會刪除 相簿原圖或舊備份中的副本。

照護通知

只有你主動開啟時，App 才會向系統要求通知權限。通知預覽不包含寵物姓名、照護名稱或 備註；可在 App 的照護通知頁關閉，也可透過系統設定調整權限。

匯出與還原

匯出會包含全部寵物資料（包括已儲存的照片）、紀錄、提醒、備註、識別碼與時間資訊，不包含上次選擇的寵物、外觀和系統通知設定。 JSON 備份沒有密碼或額外加密。請選擇你信任的儲存位置；若選擇雲端檔案服務，該服務可能 儲存副本。檢查備份只讀取你選擇的檔案；確認還原後只新增沒有衝突的資料。

分享回顧卡

回顧卡會包含所選寵物的名字、日期與畫面上的統計，不包含備註或其他寵物。照片只有在你 開啟「包含寵物照片」時才會加入；每次開啟回顧時預設關閉，分享前可查看實際圖片。你選擇分享目的地後， 收件人或服務可保存內容，處理方式依其政策。App 無法代你刪除已分享或匯出的副本。

刪除與外部副本

你可在生活時間軸檢視並確認後刪除單筆餵食、散步、體重與照護紀錄；目前沒有清除全部資料功能。 提醒可在編輯頁確認刪除，並更新通知排程；已儲存的照護紀錄會保留。 本機僅保留已刪除紀錄的類型與識別碼，以阻擋含有這些紀錄的 JSON 備份。這些標記不隨 JSON 匯出；其他安裝或較舊的裝置備份仍可能保有資料。 在 iOS 選擇「刪除 App」會移除目前裝置上的 App 與其資料；「卸載 App」會保留文件與資料。 裝置備份、你儲存的 JSON 或分享副本可能仍然存在，需在相應系統或服務管理；從舊備份 復原可能帶回當時的內容。不要把本機刪除理解成所有外部副本也被刪除。

聯絡支援

若你主動寄信至 weic200amg@gmail.com，我們會收到你寄出的聯絡方式與內容。 不要寄送完整日誌或敏感資料；我們會先以問題描述協助排查。 支援信件中的聯絡方式與內容僅用於處理你提出的問題，並在案件結束後 90 天內刪除。你可寄信至 weic200amg@gmail.com 提出刪除支援信件的請求，由營運者人工處理。 支援信箱使用 Google Gmail；Google 會依其隱私權政策處理寄件者聯絡方式、郵件內容及服務相關資料。上述 90 天期限適用於我們管理的支援信件，不代表 Google 的備份或安全紀錄採用相同期限。 若資料做法改變，將更新本頁的隱私政策 與適用揭露。

網站代管

本網站使用 GitHub Pages 代管。瀏覽網站時 GitHub 會為安全目的記錄並儲存 IP 位址；詳細處理方式見 GitHub 隱私聲明。網站沒有我們加入的追蹤碼、廣告、分析工具或資料提交表單；瀏覽支援網站不會將 App 內的日記傳送給 GitHub。

English

This privacy policy covers the Paw Notes app and support website. Effective date: 2026-10-10.

Your diary on your device

No account is required. Pet names and species, optional birthdays, joined-family dates, breed, sex and photos, feeding, walk, weight and care records, reminders and notes are stored in the app's space on your device. Your last pet selection, appearance and notification preferences are also stored locally. The current app has no developer-operated diary sync service, advertising or behavioral analytics SDK, and does not automatically upload your diary to us.

Photos you choose

Only images you choose are read through the system photo picker; the app does not request full photo-library or camera access. It saves small JPEG copies locally, removing source location and camera metadata. Removing a diary photo does not delete its original in Photos or copies in older backups.

Care notifications

The app requests notification permission only when you enable notifications. Previews omit pet names, care titles and notes. You can turn notifications off in the app's Care notifications screen or manage permission in system settings.

Export and restore

Exports include all pet details (including saved photos), records, reminders, notes, identifiers and timestamps; they exclude your last pet selection, appearance and system notification settings. JSON backups have no password or additional encryption. Choose a trusted destination; cloud file providers may store a copy. Backup checking reads the file you select. Confirmed restore adds new, conflict-free data without overwriting existing records.

Sharing recap cards

A recap card includes the selected pet's name, dates and displayed statistics, but no notes or other pets. A photo is included only when you enable Include pet photo, which starts off each time you open a recap. You can inspect the actual image before sharing. Recipients or services you choose may retain shared content under their own policies. The app cannot remove exported or shared copies for you.

Deletion and external copies

You can review and confirm deletion of individual feeding, walk, weight and care records in Life timeline. Reminders can be deleted in their editor, followed by notification reconciliation; saved care records are kept. There is no erase-all control. This installation keeps only deleted record types and IDs to block JSON backups containing them. These markers are not included in JSON exports; another installation or an older device backup may still contain the records. Deleting the app in iOS removes it and its data from that device; offloading retains documents and data. Device backups, exported JSON files and shared copies may remain and must be managed with the relevant system or service. Restoring an older backup can bring back its contents. Removing local data does not remove all external copies.

Contacting support

If you email weic200amg@gmail.com, we receive the contact details and content you send. Please describe the issue without sending your full diary or sensitive information. The contact details and content in support emails are used only to resolve your inquiry. We delete these emails within 90 days after the case is closed. You can request deletion of your support emails by contacting weic200amg@gmail.com; the operator handles these requests manually. Our support mailbox uses Google Gmail. Google processes sender contact details, email content and service-related data under its Privacy Policy. Our 90-day commitment applies to the support emails we manage; it does not set the retention period for Google’s backups or security logs. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this privacy page and in applicable disclosures.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We add no tracking code, advertising, analytics or data-submission forms to the site. Visiting the support website does not send your in-app diary to GitHub.
