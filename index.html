記錄照護，收藏日常

從今天的一餐，到一起度過的一週，記下狗狗與貓咪的小日常。

From today's meal to a week together, keep your dog or cat's everyday moments close.

陪你照顧，每一天

  • 分別記錄每隻毛孩的餵食、散步、體重與照護。
    Keep separate feeding, walk, weight and care records for each pet.
  • 查看時間軸與體重趨勢，依自己的安排設定提醒。
    Review the timeline and weight trends, and set your own care reminders.
  • 收藏七日回顧，分享前自行選擇是否加入寵物照片。
    Save a seven-day recap and choose whether to include your pet's photo before sharing.

日常，留在你的裝置

不需建立帳號。日誌保存在裝置上；可自行匯出與還原相容的 JSON 備份。通知可能受系統設定影響，提醒不提供獸醫診斷或醫療建議。

No account is required. Your diary stays on your device, with manual export and restore of compatible JSON backups. Notifications depend on system settings; reminders do not provide veterinary diagnosis or medical advice.
