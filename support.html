支援 / Support

聯絡 / Contact: weic200amg@gmail.com。 提供 App 版本、iOS 版本、重現步驟與錯誤訊息；避免傳送真實寵物備註或完整備份。 Please include the app and iOS versions, steps and error message; omit private notes and full diary exports. 本頁是 App 使用支援，不提供獸醫診斷或緊急照護服務。 This is app support, not veterinary diagnosis or emergency care.

常見問題 / Common questions
問題 / Question說明 / Answer
沒有收到通知 / Missing notifications查看照護通知開關、系統權限及專注模式；只安排下一個到期日，完成照護後再安排下一次。Check the app toggle, system permission and Focus settings; completing recurring care schedules its next occurrence.
更換手機 / Moving devices先從匯出日誌保存 JSON，再於另一裝置檢查備份、確認無衝突後還原；這不是自動同步。Export JSON, then check and restore on the other device; this is not automatic sync.
無法還原 / Restore blocked備份必須符合支援格式且不超過 16MiB；衝突時不覆寫原資料。Supported format and size are required; conflicts never overwrite existing data.
誤新增資料 / Added a record by mistake可從生活時間軸開啟紀錄，選擇刪除並確認。Open the record in Life timeline, choose Delete record and confirm.

照片與備份 / Photos and backups

移除寵物照片不會刪除相簿原圖或舊備份。包含照片的 JSON 備份同樣受 16 MiB 上限限制；匯出超過上限會顯示錯誤，不會默默省略照片。

Removing a pet photo does not delete the original or older backups. JSON backups with photos share the 16 MiB limit; oversized exports show an error instead of silently omitting photos.
